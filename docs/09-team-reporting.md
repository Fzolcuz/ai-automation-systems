# Team tasks and daily reporting

## SJF Task Board (web app, team sign-in)
* **Tasks**: each person's tasks for the day, set from the work the systems create.
* **Reports**: every evening each person reports what they did. They can **speak** it: the browser's speech-to-text (Web Speech API) writes it down in **English, Hindi or Gujarati**, and AI tidies it.
* **Questions**: decisions the team needs from the director, in one list, so nothing waits in a chat.

## Daily work log and the director's report
1. **During the day**: every finished job is logged with exact numbers: what was done, how many, where the files are, what it cost, how many retries. No passwords, keys or customer phone numbers ever go in the log.
2. **At 10:30 pm**: a scheduled AI job reads the day's log, the customer-list numbers, the chats and the new files, and drafts the director's report: short pointers, numbers first, one line per item.
3. **Approval**: nothing reaches the director until a person approves the draft.

## How it fits the systems
Lead Gen and the desks create the work, Monday holds each job with an owner and a deadline, the task board shows what each person did about it, and the nightly report sums up the day.
