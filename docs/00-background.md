# Background: Malek Media Marketing (April 2022 to May 2026)

Before SJF I spent four years as the **Lead Generation Specialist** at Malek Media Marketing, a LinkedIn and
Instagram appointment-setting agency for US and UK clients, and the only contact for every US and UK client.

MRR and ARR are the agency's client retainers on the accounts I ran, not my salary.

## Who we targeted
Executive, leadership and business coaches; consultants and fractional advisors; founders and CEOs of AI, tech
and SaaS startups; authors and speakers. Titles from CEO, president, VP and director down to team lead and
manager, depending on the client. United States, United Kingdom, Europe and the UAE. Company size 1 to 10 scored
best, 11 to 50 was fine, over 50 lost points. Kept out: wellness and spiritual coaches, competing agencies and
LinkedIn coaches, sales trainers and appointment setters, local-only businesses. Every client had a written
ideal-customer profile with their own rules (for example "VPs score higher than directors").

## The system I built there
1. **Find**: LinkedIn Sales Navigator saved searches per client, filtered by title, seniority, industry, company size and location.
2. **Pull**: PhantomBuster exported each search into a spreadsheet (starter plan, about $69 a month).
3. **Clean**: a Google Sheets pipeline: raw list, cleaned list, batches.
4. **Score**: ChatGPT gave every profile points from fixed rules plus the client's own rules, with a reason; approved or rejected, the best marked "super ideal".
5. **Write**: five message styles matched to the person (for example peer-to-peer for founders who coach).
6. **Send**: connection request, a thank-you on accept, the main message 2 days later, follow-ups at 3 and 5 days, with testimonials and a Calendly booking link. Dux-Soup did the sending.
7. **Track**: one Google Sheet per client, copied from a locked template, with my Apps Script: status menus, a time-stamped history ("request sent 3 hours ago"), who changed what, colour alerts. A new client was set up in seconds.
8. **Protect**: each client's LinkedIn ran in its own Chrome profile from one fixed location.

Tools: Sales Navigator, PhantomBuster, Dux-Soup, ChatGPT, Google Sheets with Apps Script, Calendly, HubSpot.

What I learnt: we pushed volume; after LinkedIn's late-2025 crackdown on automation, I now run at human pace with
varied timing and research every profile first, which is how the SJF Lead Gen system works.
